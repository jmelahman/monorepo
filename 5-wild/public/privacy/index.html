Privacy Policy for 5 Wild

Last updated 27 September 2026

5 Wild does not collect or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. The only thing the game will ever send is an anonymous replay of your runs, and only if you agree to it. This policy describes what the game stores, where it stays, and exactly what that replay contains.

What the game stores

The game saves your progress on your own device so that closing it does not lose a run. That save holds only:

  • the state of the run in progress: the board, your score, gold, and relics;
  • your record across runs, such as best score and furthest stage reached;
  • your settings, including sound, music, and display preferences.

On the web this is stored in your browser's local storage. In the Android app it is stored in the app's private storage. In both cases it never leaves the device, and it is not readable by us or by anyone else.

Sharing run replays

At the end of a run the game asks once whether you want to share your run results to help improve it. Nothing is sent unless you say yes, and ignoring the question counts as no. You can change your answer at any time from the ⓘ button on the title screen or from the pause menu. Turning it off also discards any replays still waiting to be sent.

If you say yes, each finished run sends:

  • the moves you made: the words you guessed and what you bought, sold, or used, in order;
  • the run's random seed, difficulty level, and word-list language, which are what make those moves replayable;
  • how the run ended, and how many runs you had started before it;
  • the version of the game you were playing.

It does not include any identifier for you or your device, your settings, or the time you played. The server records only the day each replay arrived. The replays are hosted by Cloudflare, which necessarily sees your IP address when your device connects; the server uses it only to limit how often it accepts replays and does not store it. Replays are used only to tune the game's balance and are never sold or shared.

What the game does not do

  • It does not collect your name, email address, location, or contacts.
  • It does not use advertising or analytics software.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites.
  • It does not send your gameplay, scores, or settings anywhere, except the run replays described above, and only if you agree.

The Android app uses its internet permission only to send those replays. The word lists and all other content ship inside the app and it plays fully offline.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored locally, you remove it yourself: uninstall the Android app, or clear its storage from your device's app settings. On the web, clear site data for this domain in your browser. Shared replays carry no identifier, so there is no way to tell which ones were yours, and so no way for us to find and delete them.

Changes

If a future version of the game handles data differently, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date above will change with it.

  • 27 September 2026: added optional, anonymous sharing of run replays (see Sharing run replays). Nothing is sent unless you agree.

Contact

You can raise questions about this policy at github.com/jmelahman/5-wild/issues.

The game's "Report an issue" button opens a new issue there, with the game's version, platform and language filled in. Nothing is sent until you choose to post it, and anything posted there is public.
