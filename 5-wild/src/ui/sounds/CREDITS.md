# Sound credits

Every recording here is released under CC0 1.0 (public domain): most from
[Kenney](https://kenney.nl)'s asset packs, and the few that needed brass or a
bell Kenney has nothing like, from [Freesound](https://freesound.org). No
attribution is required; this file is here so each one can be traced back to
its source, and credits the Freesound authors anyway.

Each was trimmed of leading silence, peak-normalized to -1dBFS and encoded
as Vorbis at q5, so that its level is set in `LEVELS` in `src/ui/audio.ts`
rather than by however hot it was mastered.
