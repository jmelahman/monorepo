# Generated assets

Models from AI services rather than our own scripts, each brought to scale by
`scripts/blender/generated.py`. Record each one here.

## monolith.glb

- Tripo H3.1 text-to-3D through fal (`tripo3d/h3.1/text-to-3d`), request
  `01a19554-3895-7de2-8ad4-966c33ced0af`, on a paid fal account, 2026-10-31.
- Reduced from 1,940,491 to about 180,000 triangles and scaled to 31.5 m.
- Prompt: "A colossal brutalist memorial monolith, in the style of a Yugoslav spomenik: a
  tall canted slab of raw weathered board-marked concrete, cast in stacked slightly offset
  sections, with a deep vertical cleft crossed by rusted steel rungs, carved rectangular
  coffers and vertical ribs, bolted steel straps, a broken top with exposed rebar, standing
  on a wide stepped plinth. Whole object, centred, no ground, no background."
- Terms: fal tags the model "Commercial use". Its terms put use of output at the customer's
  risk, may not make it unique, and bar training AI models on it. Tripo's help centre says
  paid users hold the rights to their output. Tripo's own terms of service could not be read
  (403). Read them, and ask whether AI output can sit under the project's licence, before a
  public release.
