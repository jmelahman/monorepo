# Player bike: 56 cm aero road bike

The whole bike is built procedurally by `build_bike.py`, with no downloaded or
imported assets. The geometry constants are at the top of the script.

```sh
blender -b -P build_bike.py                        # full build + renders (~5.5 min, 24-core CPU)
blender -b -P build_bike.py -- --no-render         # mesh, FBX and textures only
blender -b -P build_bike.py -- --quick             # low-sample renders for iteration
blender -b -P build_bike.py -- --out /some/dir     # write outputs elsewhere
```

Outputs, all in one folder:

- `bike.fbx` and `bike.blend`
- `T_*.png` textures
- `render_*.png` renders at 1600×900
- `stats.json`, holding the triangle counts and pivots

`bike.fbx` (89 MB) and `bike.blend` (39 MB) are regenerated by the script and
are not committed.

## Units and axes

- Blender: metres, +X forward, +Z up. The drive side is −Y. Both tyres touch Z = 0.
- FBX: UnitScaleFactor 100, so 1 BU becomes 100 UE cm. Export settings: Z up, tangent space on, face smoothing.
- In UE, import with **Convert Scene** on and import uniform scale 1.
  - Blender +X stays UE +X (forward).
  - The drive side lands on UE +Y (right).
- All transforms are applied except the part pivots. There are no negative scales, and normals face outward.

## Objects

The empty **`Bike`** sits on the ground directly under the BB, at (0, 0, 0). Every part below is parented to it.

| Object | Pivot (m, Blender) | Rotation | Spin/steer axis | Tris | Contents |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Frame` | 0, 0, 0 (ground under BB) | identity | n/a | 447,882 | frame, seatpost, saddle, rear caliper, FD, RD (cage posed for the chain line) |
| `Fork` | 0.41193, 0, 0.76228 (head tube centre, on the steering axis) | Y −17° | local **+Z** = steering axis (−0.2924, 0, 0.9563) | 383,060 | fork, bar/stem, hoods and levers, front caliper |
| `WheelFront` | 0.5863, 0, 0.339 (axle centre) | identity | local **Y** | 200,632 | rim, tyre, hub, spokes, rotor, thru-axle |
| `WheelRear` | −0.40195, 0, 0.339 (axle centre) | identity | local **Y** | 349,324 | as front, plus the cassette |
| `Cranks` | 0, 0, 0.269 (BB centre) | identity | local **Y** | 52,992 | both arms, spider, 52/36 rings, BB cups |
| `PedalR` | 0.1725, −0.073, 0.269 (spindle) | identity | local **Y** | 5,616 | drive-side pedal |
| `PedalL` | −0.1725, 0.073, 0.269 (spindle) | identity | local **Y** | 5,616 | non-drive pedal |
| `Chain` | 0, 0, 0 | identity | static | 95,040 | 110 links, routed on the 52T ring and the 17T cog |

**Total: 1,540,162 triangles.** This fits Nanite with no LODs needed.

Notes for rigging:

- The cranks rest with the drive-side arm pointing forward (+X).
- To pedal, rotate `Cranks` about Y. Move each pedal with the crank arm by attaching it to the crank's pedal eye, at radius 0.1725 m. Counter-rotate the pedal so it stays level.
- Steering is a rotation of `Fork` about its own Z axis. The front wheel should follow it, for example by attaching the wheel to the fork.
- The chain is static and the drivetrain is modelled on a single gear. To animate the drive, scroll the chain material or swap in a spline.

## Materials

Each part uses one material slot per material.

| Material | Used on | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `M_FramePaint` | frame, fork | deep midnight-blue metallic with a clear coat |
| `M_Decal` | down tube and seat tube stripes | orange, no wordmark; built as separate geometry bands |
| `M_Carbon` | rims, bar/stem, crank arms, RD cage | 2×2 twill. Textures: `T_Carbon_BaseColor.png`, `T_Carbon_Normal.png` |
| `M_Rubber` | tyres | matte with a tan sidewall and a subtle file tread. Textures: `T_Rubber_BaseColor.png`, `T_Rubber_Normal.png` |
| `M_Alloy` | spokes, rotors, cassette, chain, chainrings, bolts, axles | brushed metal |
| `M_BlackPlastic` | hoods, pedal bodies, saddle, pulleys, caliper bodies | satin black |
| `M_BarTape` | bar tape | matte, with a procedural wrap bump that follows the bar's UVs |

Texture notes:

- Textures are at most 2048 px.
- The normal maps are **OpenGL (Y+)**. In UE, tick **Flip Green Channel** on the normal-map textures.
- The paint, alloy, plastic and bar-tape materials are flat values. Rebuild them as UE material instances from the parameters in `build_bike.py`, in the `make_materials` function.

## Deviations from the spec

- The cassette is merged into `WheelRear`, as asked.
- The saddle and seatpost belong to `Frame`, as asked.
- The chainrings use `M_Alloy`, and the RD pulleys and inner parts use `M_BlackPlastic`. The spec doesn't assign these parts.
- An extra render, `render_chase_cam.png`, shows the in-game chase-camera view.
