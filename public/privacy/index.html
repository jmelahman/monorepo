Privacy Policy for 5 Wild

Last updated 15 August 2026

5 Wild does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party services of any kind. This policy describes what the game stores and where it stays.

What the game stores

The game saves your progress on your own device so that closing it does not lose a run. That save holds only:

  • the state of the run in progress: the board, your score, gold, and relics;
  • your record across runs, such as best score and furthest stage reached;
  • your settings, including sound, music, and display preferences.

On the web this is stored in your browser's local storage. In the Android app it is stored in the app's private storage. In both cases it never leaves the device, and it is not readable by us or by anyone else.

What the game does not do

  • It does not collect your name, email address, location, or contacts.
  • It does not use advertising or analytics software.
  • It does not track you across apps or websites.
  • It does not send your gameplay, scores, or settings anywhere.

The Android app requests internet permission because of the framework it is built on, but the game does not use it: the word lists and all other content ship inside the app and it plays fully offline.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Because everything is stored locally, you remove it yourself: uninstall the Android app, or clear its storage from your device's app settings. On the web, clear site data for this domain in your browser. There is nothing stored elsewhere for us to delete.

Changes

If a future version of the game handles data differently, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date above will change with it.

Contact

You can raise questions about this policy at github.com/jmelahman/5-wild/issues.
